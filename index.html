Statutory Rule Book

Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011

Rule 6 — Mandatory Declarations on Package

Every package must bear legible, prominent declarations including: Manufacturer Name & Address, Country of Origin, Common/Generic Name, Net Quantity, Month & Year of Manufacture, Maximum Retail Price (MRP incl. of all taxes), Consumer Care Details, and Net Quantity Declaration.

Rule 7 — Height & Width Ratio of Numerals & Letters

Minimum height of numerals and letters depending on net quantity (e.g., ≤50g/ml: 1mm, >50g/ml to 200g/ml: 2mm, >200g/ml to 1kg/l: 4mm, >1kg/l: 6mm). Width ratio to height must not be less than 1:3.

Rule 18 — Provisions Relating to Wholesale / Retail Sale

No package shall be sold at a price higher than the Maximum Retail Price (MRP) declared on the package.
